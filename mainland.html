Mainland Company Formation

What is a Mainland Licence?

A mainland company is a company licensed by the Department of Economic Development (DED) in any of the Emirate of the United Arab Emirates. United Arab Emirates consists of seven emirates, viz. Abu Dhabi, Dubai, Sharjah, Ajman, Umm Al Quwain, Ras Al Khaimah and Fujairah. Each of these Emirate has its own Department of Economic Developments (DEDs). You have the advantage of obtaining a licence in any of these emirates.

Why would you obtain a Mainland Licence?

Amongst the many benefits of obtaining a mainland licence, the most important one is the ability to have 100% foreign ownership, which was recently introduced by the Government.

  • Choice of location of your office and no trade restrictions.
  • No minimum or fixed share capital required.
  • Advantage of access to government contracts and business.
  • Advantage to obtain and bid government projects.
  • 100% ownership for a host of activities.
  • Preferential treatment for opening bank accounts in the UAE.
  • Easy processing of visa for employment.
  • Can trade domestically as well as import/export.

Name the activity, our Mainland licences cover them.

You have the benefit of choosing from over 2500 business activities. Whether you are setting up a gym or a grocery store, or you need to start a major import-export company with offices across the globe, the same is covered under the Mainland licence. You also have the advantage of bundling different related activities in the same licence, to avoid multiple trade licences. Certain activities would need approval from the relevant authorities/government bodies. We will make all arrangements to assist you to obtain these approvals too. We would be with you from start until completion.

Types of Licences

Whatever is your activity and how you would like to structure your company, the Department of Economic Development has the right type of licence for you.

Professional Licence

A professional licence is required when you are in the service industry. Services and Consultancies fall within this licence. Whether you are providing financial, marketing, IT or any other services and/or consultancy, you could obtain a professional licence to carry on your business seamlessly.

Commercial Licence

A Commercial licence is required for carrying on all trading activities, both inland as well as import/export of goods. Every possible trade is covered under the various activities listed by DED under the Mainland licencing. Trading in gold, foodstuff, oil, chemicals and the list goes onâ€¦.

Industrial Licence

As the name suggests, Industrial licence needs to be obtained for all kinds of manufacturing activities. You may set up a factory for readymade garments or a unit to process food related products, the same would be covered under industrial licencing.

Different structures as you desire

We can organise the appropriate licence with the required activity in the right structure of your choice. The various structures are:

Limited Liability Company (LLC)

LLC is the most predominant type of business setup in the UAE Mainland. The minimum number of shareholders required to form an LLC is 2 while the maximum is 50. The company can now be wholly owned by foreign owner. No local sponsorship is required. Majority of the companies registered in the UAE are LLC companies.

Branch of a Foreign Company

If you wish to retain the ownership of the company as a branch of your existing company anywhere in the world, the same is permissible. The foreign company, in this case, would be the shareholder of the UAE company. This will enable you to retain the brand and the ownership under the parent company.

Sole Proprietorship Company

These are single owner companies which is owned by a single individual. Primarily used by single owner professional, service industry and consultant.
